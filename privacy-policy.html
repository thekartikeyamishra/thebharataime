  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Lodge a complaint about our data practices
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate a person to exercise rights in the event of death or incapacity

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at workmailkartikeya@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.
