The Problem I Built This to Solve

India runs 1,500+ central and state government schemes worth over ₹100 billion annually. PM Kisan sends ₹6,000/year to small farmers. Ayushman Bharat provides ₹5L health cover to over 55 crore people. PM Awas gives ₹1.3–1.5L for housing.

But here's the reality: 80%+ of eligible Indians either don't know they qualify, don't know how to apply, or apply incorrectly and get rejected. The information is buried in English PDFs on .gov.in portals that load slowly on 2G connections.

The people who need this most — farmers in Gorakhpur, daily workers in Patna, women in rural Rajasthan — are the least equipped to discover what's owed to them. That's the problem I'm solving.

What TheBharatAI Does

The Bharat Haq Engine™ is a free, client-side AI eligibility matcher. You enter your state, occupation, age, category, and income range. In 60 seconds, you get:

  • Every active scheme you likely qualify for (closed schemes are permanently hidden)
  • Total benefit value you're potentially eligible for
  • Required documents checklist for each scheme
  • Direct links to official .gov.in apply portals
  • Freshness dates so you know how recently each scheme was verified

No login. No Aadhaar. No personal data stored on our servers. The engine runs entirely in your browser. DPDP Act 2023 compliant.

Data Accuracy & Freshness System

Every scheme in the database has a lastVerified date and a freshness tag. The system works like this:

  • Active: Scheme is live and fully verified against official portal
  • Modified: Scheme criteria or amount has changed — shown with update note
  • Closed: Scheme discontinued — permanently hidden and never shown

I manually verify every scheme against official government portals each month. New schemes are added within 7 days of launch. Closed schemes are removed immediately. If you spot an error, every scheme card has a "Report Wrong Info" button — I fix it within 72 hours.
